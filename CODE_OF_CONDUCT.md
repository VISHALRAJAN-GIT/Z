# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to build something difficult and honest. That requires people
who are technically rigorous and civil.

Expected behaviour:

- Criticise the work, not the person.
- State a measurement and the method that produced it. Separate what you observed
  from what you inferred.
- Accept correction gracefully. Everyone here will get things wrong.
- Give credit where it is due.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Presenting a result you did not measure.
- Claiming an unverified step works because it seemed to.

## Reporting

Report privately to the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer will acknowledge, assess, and act. Retaliation against a reporter is
itself a violation.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone
is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Warnings for a first substantiated violation. Escalation to a temporary or
permanent ban for continued or severe violations. The maintainer is the final
authority on enforcement.
